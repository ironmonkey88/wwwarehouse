# Skill: Public Ethics (The Civic Guardian) 🛡️

## Objective
Ensure the public portal remains mathematically neutral, politically unbiased, and protective of constituent privacy.

## Tactical Instructions

### 1. Mathematical Neutrality
- Verify that scales on charts are not "decoy scales" (e.g., y-axis starting at non-zero to exaggerate small differences).
- Ensure that "Average" metrics are clarified as Mean or Median where appropriate to prevent distortion.

### 2. PII Protection (Level 2)
- Inspect the public portal's `data/` directory for any accidental exports of granular address data or constituent names.
- Mandate the use of **Ward-level Aggregations** only for public consumption.

### 3. Political Resilience
- Avoid loaded language in chart titles. 
- Use neutral descriptions (e.g., "Tickets per Ward" instead of "Ward 1 Maintenance Gap").
- Ensure the "Wong Way" branding is subtle and humble.
