# 🧬 Design Philosophy: The Sovereign Engine

The **Somerville Civic Pulse** is not just a dashboard; it is the manifestation of the **Sovereign Engine**. We follow the "Wong Way" of agentic engineering—where the workforce is autonomous, the pipelines are serverless, and the audit trail is immutable.

---

## 👷 The Workforce Forge

Our operations are orchestrated by a specialized workforce, each bound by a distinct genetic profile:

*   **🏛️ The Architect**: Ensures structural integrity and ADR-first engineering.
*   **🍶 The Refiner**: Masters of the Medallion refinery and SQL purity.
*   **🛠️ The Assembler**: Orchestrators of the "One-Word Ship" and release cycles.
*   **📊 The Mayor**: Governors of the PRD, mission status, and communication.
*   **🛡️ The Witness**: Validators of the "Testing Trinity" (Size, Shape, Sample).

---

## 🤝 The Master SDLC

We adhere to a declarative 7-stage pipeline to ensure zero-friction from Idea to Production:

1.  **Idea** → **Bead Origin** (Task Graph Initiation)
2.  **PRD Certification** (User Impact Definition)
3.  **Technical Spec & ADR** (Structural Blueprinting)
4.  **Implementation Handshake** (Explicit Approval)
5.  **Refinery Execution** (Refiner/Coder Implementation)
6.  **Witness Audit** (Triple-Seal Quality Gate)
7.  **Mission Closure** (Production Shipment)

---

## 💴 Token Sovereignty

To maintain a "Zero-Idle" cost profile, we optimize model inference through a tiered strategy:

*   **Cloud Tier (Gemini/Claude)**: Reserved for high-order Strategy, Architecture, and complex ADRs.
*   **Local Tier (Ollama/Qwen)**: Leveraged for repetitive Tasks, Formatting, and Code Review.
